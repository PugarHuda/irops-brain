Southwest, December 2022

In the week before Christmas 2022 Southwest cancelled nearly 17,000 flights. Its crew system lost track of where crews and aircraft were and how they connected. This replay loads the airline's real schedule for 21–23 December from US DOT data, and lets three agents work the storm from the graph.

    Ask the graph

    Does it match what really happened?

    US DOT records how many minutes of each flight's delay came from its aircraft arriving late. We seed the graph with only each flight's own weather, air-traffic and carrier delay, let it propagate along aircraft rotations, and compare.

    The agents' own calls, against what the airline really did

    Each scenario is run as the agents would at its clock. A cancel is borne out if the airline also cancelled the flight or flew it 3h+ late. Projected 3h+ delays (the 14 CFR 260.6 refund line) are checked against real arrivals, and compared with the base rate among every flight downstream of the known delays.

    Use the same tools from your own agent

    The read-only tool catalogue is also an MCP server: the same human-written Cypher, id resolution and paths, for Southwest (WN) and Alaska (AS), plus the decision log, the cited rules and past lessons as resources, and a briefing prompt. Add it to Claude Code:

    claude mcp add --transport http irops-brain /mcp/

    Flight data: US DOT BTS On-Time Performance, Dec 2022 (public). Crews and bookings are synthetic, generated on the real schedule. Rules: 14 CFR Part 117. Not affiliated with any airline.
